Learning outcome 01 / History

Explain key moments in the historical development of AI and ML and relate them to modern AI systems.

My evidence

AI developed from early ideas about machine intelligence into systems that learn from data. Turing’s imitation game (1950) explored whether machines could communicate like humans, while the Dartmouth workshop (1956) established AI as a research field. Early systems relied on programmed rules, but their limitations contributed to AI winters. Statistical machine learning gained prominence in the 1990, followed by AlexNet’s deep-learning breakthrough in 2012 and transformers in 2017. These advances helped enable ChatGPT, launched in 2022. Unlike ELIZA’s scripted pattern matching, ChatGPT uses a trained neural network to generate responses one token—a word or word fragment—at a time. Attention helps it use relevant context when generating explanations, summaries, and code. This illustrates the shift from manually written conversational rules to learned language patterns, although fluent responses can still contain errors. My Jay project connects this history to a practical AI companion designed for a Raspberry Pi 5. It combines pretrained Gemma and Qwen models for conversation with programmed rules for selecting models and handling tool commands. This reflects both early rule-based approaches and modern machine learning. Jay also retrieves stored memories to personalize responses, supplying context without retraining its models.
