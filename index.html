About Me

Computer Engineering Student & Software Developer

I’m Josh Anicette, a junior studying Computer Engineering at Drexel University with a minor in Software Engineering. I’m taking this course because I’ve started building AI into projects that combine software and hardware, and I want to understand the ideas behind the tools I use. While building Jay, my local AI companion on a Raspberry Pi, I connected a language model to a conversation system and text-to-speech. Hearing Jay respond made AI feel like something I could build and improve, while also showing me how much more I have to learn. I hope this course helps me make better decisions about when and how to use AI in my projects. I’m most curious about the objective on generative AI and large language models because it connects directly to Jay. One concern I have is that a model can give an answer that sounds confident even when it’s wrong, which matters if someone comes to rely on a tool I build. Outside of class, I play basketball and soccer, read Marvel comics, build LEGO car and Marvel sets, and listen to hip hop, R&B, and blues on vinyl.
